    Nutrition guide

    Meat and eggs are the base. Carbs and fats are add-on slots, about 100 calories each.

    Daily targets
    Brad
    18 oz raw meat (4 breakfast, 7 lunch, 7 dinner) · 4 eggs · 10 add-on slots (about 1,000 kcal)
    Sarah
    14 oz raw meat (4 breakfast, 5 lunch, 5 dinner) · 2 eggs · 7 add-on slots (about 700 kcal)

    Eggs can swap for meat: each egg not eaten is +1 oz raw meat. Brad with no eggs is 22 oz; Sarah is 16 oz.

    Ground rules
    • All meat is weighed raw, before cooking.
    • Slots move. Spend them at breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks as long as the day's total lands.
    • Cooking fat counts. Butter, oil, cream or melted cheese used to cook comes out of your fat slots.
    • Free, unlimited: non-starchy veggies, zero-calorie seasonings, black coffee, water, yellow mustard, hot sauce, lemon juice, vinegar.
